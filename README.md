# SEIN-SLAM
Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) have emerged as a prominent technique for three-dimensional (3D) scene reconstruction. However, conventional NeRF models lack the capacity for semantic understanding. To address this limitation, we propose Semantic-Enhanced Implicit Neural Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SEIN-SLAM), a novel simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) framework that integrates semantic awareness into the NeRF representation. Our approach combines implicit neural scene representations with sparse semantic annotations to achieve accurate 3D scene understanding and robust camera localization. By embedding semantic prediction directly into the NeRF architecture, the model learns semantic information directly from visual observations, leveraging NeRF's inherent multi-view consistency to propagate and refine semantic labels across views—even in the presence of annotation noise—thus improving the robustness of semantic segmentation. Furthermore, SEIN-SLAM incorporates a multi-resolution hash grid to efficiently encode both geometric and semantic features, enabling high-fidelity scene representation while maintaining computational efficiency. Extensive experiments on multiple benchmarks validate the effectiveness of our method in semantic scene reconstruction and camera tracking. Results show that SEIN-SLAM not only generates geometrically accurate reconstructions but also delivers consistent semantic segmentation across diverse environments, demonstrating competitive performance compared to existing NeRF-based dense SLAM systems.

Keywords: Field Robots, Dense semantic mapping, Neural implicit representation, Implicit scene reconstruction
